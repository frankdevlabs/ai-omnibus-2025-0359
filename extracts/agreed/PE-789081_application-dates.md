<a id="point-30--article-111-transition"></a>
## Point (30) — Article 111 amended (AI systems already placed on the market; grace periods)

(30) Article 111 is amended as follows:

(a) paragraph 2 is replaced by the following:

> ‘2. Without prejudice to the application of Article 5 as referred to in Article 113, third paragraph, point (a), this Regulation shall apply to operators of high-risk AI systems, other than the systems referred to in paragraph 1 of this Article, that have been placed on the market or put into service before the date of application of Chapter III and corresponding obligations referred to in Article 113, only if, as from that date, those systems are subject to significant changes in their designs. In any case, the providers and deployers of high-risk AI systems intended to be used by public authorities shall take the necessary steps to comply with the requirements and obligations laid down in this Regulation by 2 August 2030.;’

(b) the following paragraph 4 is added:

> ‘4. Providers of AI systems, including general-purpose AI systems, generating synthetic audio, image, video or text content, that have been placed on the market before 2 August 2026 shall take the necessary steps in order to comply with Article 50(2) by 2 December 2026.’

▸ (a) is materially as the Commission's [point (30)(a)](../commission/COM-2025-836_application-dates.md#point-30--article-111-transition) — the grace period re-keyed to "the date of application of Chapter III", which under the agreed [point (31)](#point-31--article-113-application-dates) is now a **fixed date** rather than the Commission's conditional mechanism; the citation is restyled "Article 113, third paragraph, point (a)" and per [recital (21)](./PE-789081_recitals.md#recital-21) the grace period applies per **type and model** of system. (b) is the **Article 50(2) watermarking transition, shortened by the co-legislators**: generative systems already on the market before 2 August 2026 must comply by **2 December 2026** — a four-month transition instead of the Commission's six months (2 February 2027); cf. [recital (20)](./PE-789081_recitals.md#recital-20) and the [transparency slice](./PE-789081_transparency-art50.md).

<a id="point-31--article-113-application-dates"></a>
## Point (31) — Article 113 amended (application dates: Article 5 ban; fixed high-risk dates)

(31) Article 113 is amended as follows:

(-a) in the third paragraph, point (a) is replaced by the following:

> (a) Chapters I and II shall apply from 2 February 2025, except for Article 5(1), first subparagraph, points (ba) and (bb), Article 5(1a) and Article 5(1b) which shall apply from 2 December 2026 ;’

(a) in the third paragraph, point (c) is replaced by the following:

> ‘Chapter III, Sections 1, 2, and 3, with the exception of Article 6(5), shall apply
>
> (i) on 2 December 2027 as regards AI systems classified as high-risk pursuant to Article 6(2) and Annex III, and
>
> (ii) on 2 August 2028 as regards AI systems classified as high-risk pursuant to Article 6(1) and Annex I.;’

(b) in the third paragraph, point (d) is added:

> ‘(d) Articles 102 to 110 shall apply from [the date of entry into force of this amending Regulation].’;’

*Transcription notes (not source text): (i) sub-point (-a)'s replacement opens without a quote mark and ends "2 December 2026 ;’" with a space before the semicolon — as printed; (ii) sub-point (a)'s replacement text omits the "(c)" point label — as printed; (iii) the bracketed placeholder "[the date of entry into force of this amending Regulation]" is undecided in the source and reproduced exactly (the Commission text's placeholder read "entry into application").*

▸ Replaces the Commission's [point (31)](../commission/COM-2025-836_application-dates.md#point-31--article-113-application-dates) conditional application mechanism (application 6/12 months after a Commission decision confirming support measures, with longstop dates) with **fixed dates** — the Council-mandate approach: Chapter III, Sections 1–3 (except Article 6(5)) apply **on 2 December 2027 for Annex III high-risk systems (Article 6(2))** and **on 2 August 2028 for Annex I high-risk systems (Article 6(1))**, replacing the current AI Act dates of 2 August 2026/2027. Per [recital (22)](./PE-789081_recitals.md#recital-22), the Commission must instead ensure that support measures are in place in due time. New sub-point **(-a)** — added by the co-legislators — sets the application date of the new [Article 5 prohibitions (point (5a))](./PE-789081_prohibitions-art5.md#point-5a--article-5-prohibitions) at **2 December 2026** (the rest of Chapters I and II continue to apply from 2 February 2025). Sub-point (b) keeps the immediate application of the sectoral amendments (Articles 102–110 AI Act), now from entry into **force** of the amending Regulation.
