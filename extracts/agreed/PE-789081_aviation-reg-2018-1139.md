All seven points are **unchanged in substance from the Commission proposal**: each inserts a
materially identical new paragraph 3 into seven empowerment articles of Regulation (EU) 2018/1139
(the EASA Basic Regulation), requiring that the high-risk AI requirements of Chapter III,
Section 2, of the AI Act be taken into account when the Commission adopts implementing acts
(points (1), (2), (4), (6), (7)) or delegated acts (points (3), (5)) concerning AI systems that
are safety components. (The amendments to the **Machinery** Regulation (EU) 2023/1230, added by
the co-legislators as a new Article 2a of the omnibus, are in the
[sectoral slice](./PE-789081_sectoral-annexi-interaction.md#article-2a--machinery-regulation).)

## Article 2 — Amendments to Regulation (EU) 2018/1139

Regulation (EU) 2018/1139 is amended as follows:

<a id="art2-point-1--article-27"></a>
### Point (1) — Article 27 (implementing acts)

(1) in Article 27, the following paragraph is added:

> ‘3. Without prejudice to paragraph 2, when adopting implementing acts pursuant to paragraph 1 concerning Artificial Intelligence systems which are safety components within the meaning of Regulation (EU) 2024/1689 of the European Parliament and of the Council[^1], the requirements set out in Chapter III, Section 2, of that Regulation shall be taken into account.;

▸ As the Commission's [point (1)](../commission/COM-2025-836_aviation-reg-2018-1139.md#art2-point-1--article-27); the closing punctuation is ".;" without a quote mark in this rendering.

<a id="art2-point-2--article-31"></a>
### Point (2) — Article 31 (implementing acts)

> ‘3. Without prejudice to paragraph 2, when adopting implementing acts pursuant to paragraph 1 concerning Artificial Intelligence systems which are safety components within the meaning of Regulation (EU) 2024/1689 of the European Parliament and of the Council, the requirements set out in Chapter III, Section 2, of that Regulation shall be taken into account.;’

▸ Same insertion into Article 31 (air operations implementing acts) — unchanged from the Commission text.

<a id="art2-point-3--article-32"></a>
### Point (3) — Article 32 (delegated acts)

When adopting delegated acts pursuant to paragraph 1 concerning Artificial Intelligence systems which are safety components within the meaning of Regulation (EU) 2024/1689 of the European Parliament and of the Council (*), the requirements set out in Chapter III, Section 2, of that Regulation shall be taken into account.;’
