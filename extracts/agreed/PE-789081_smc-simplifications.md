<a id="point-3--article-3-sme-smc-definitions"></a>
## Point (3) — Article 3 amended ('safety component' definition; new SME/SMC definitions (14a) and (14b))

(3) Article 3 is amended as follows:

Point (14) is amended as follows:

> (14) ‘safety component’ means a component of a product or of an AI system which fulfils a safety function for that product or AI system, or the failure or malfunctioning of which endangers the health and safety of persons or property; for the purposes of this definition, a component fulfils a safety function where its intended purpose is to prevent or mitigate risks to health and safety of persons or property;

‘(a) the following points (14a) and (14b) are inserted:

> (14a) micro, small and medium-sized enterprise (‘SME’) means a micro, small or medium-sized enterprise as defined in Article 2 of the Annex to Commission Recommendation 2003/361/EC;
>
> (14b) small mid-cap enterprise (‘SMC’) means a small mid-cap enterprise as defined in point (2) of the Annex to Commission Recommendation (EU) 2025/1099;’

*Transcription notes (not source text): (i) the sub-instructions are irregularly formatted in the source — the point (14) amendment carries no letter, while the insertion instruction is lettered "(a)" and preceded by a stray opening quote mark — as printed; (ii) the replacement definition is printed without quotation marks.*

▸ Extends the Commission's [point (3)](../commission/COM-2025-836_smc-simplifications.md#point-3--article-3-sme-smc-definitions). The SME/SMC definitions (14a)/(14b) are as proposed (with point (14b) now ending in a semicolon). **New versus the Commission proposal:** the co-legislators also rewrite the **'safety component' definition in Article 3, point (14)** — adding that a component "fulfils a safety function where its intended purpose is to prevent or mitigate risks to health and safety of persons or property", narrowing the classification gateway of Article 6(1) (cf. [recital (4a)](./PE-789081_recitals.md#recital-4a)); the operative classification clarifications are in [point (5b)](./PE-789081_high-risk-classification-art6.md#point-5b--article-6-safety-components). The citation oddity of the baseline recital (Recommendation "2025/3500/EC" in the recital versus (EU) 2025/1099 in the definition) persists in the agreed [recital (4)](./PE-789081_recitals.md#recital-4).

<a id="point-8--article-11-1-technical-documentation"></a>
## Point (8) — Article 11(1), second subparagraph replaced (simplified technical documentation)

(8) in Article 11(1), the second subparagraph is replaced by the following:

> ‘That technical documentation shall be drawn up in such a way as to demonstrate that the high-risk AI system complies with the requirements set out in this Section and to provide national competent authorities and notified bodies with the necessary information in a clear and comprehensive form to assess the compliance of the AI system with those requirements. It shall contain, at a minimum, the elements set out in Annex IV. SMEs, including start-ups, and SMCs, may provide the elements of the technical documentation specified in Annex IV in a simplified manner. To that end, the Commission shall establish a simplified technical documentation form targeted at the needs of SMEs, including start-ups, and SMCs. Where an SME, including a start-up, or an SMC, opts to provide the information required in Annex IV in a simplified manner, it shall use the form referred to in this paragraph. Notified bodies shall accept the form for the purposes of the conformity assessment.;’

▸ As the Commission's [point (8)](../commission/COM-2025-836_smc-simplifications.md#point-8--article-11-1-technical-documentation), with the enumeration order normalised to "SMEs, including start-ups, and SMCs" throughout (the Commission text wrote "SMCs and SMEs, including start-ups").

<a id="point-21--article-63-1-sme-qms"></a>
## Point (21) — Article 63(1) replaced (simplified quality management for SMEs)

(21) Article 63(1) is replaced by the following:

> ‘1. SMEs, including start-ups, may comply with certain elements of the quality management system required by Article 17 in a simplified manner, provided that they do not have partner enterprises or linked enterprises within the meaning of Recommendation 2003/361/EC. For that purpose, the Commission shall develop guidelines on the elements of the quality management system which may be complied with in a simplified manner considering the needs of SMEs, without affecting the level of protection or the need for compliance with the requirements in respect of high-risk AI systems.’;’

▸ As the Commission's [point (21)](../commission/COM-2025-836_smc-simplifications.md#point-21--article-63-1-sme-qms) — the extension from microenterprises to all SMEs, including start-ups — **but with a new proviso added by the co-legislators**: the simplified route is available only to SMEs without partner or linked enterprises within the meaning of Recommendation 2003/361/EC (i.e. standalone SMEs). Still not extended to SMCs.

<a id="point-28--article-96-1-guidelines"></a>
## Point (28) — Article 96(1) amended (Commission guidelines)

(28) Article 96 is amended as follows:

(a) in paragraph 1, the following point (g) is added:

> ‘(g) the practical implementation of Articles 8(2), 9(10), and 17(3) in line with the principle of complementarity and proportionality, with a view to ensuring consistency, avoiding duplication and minimising additional burdens when complying with the requirements of this Regulation and the requirements of the Union harmonisation legislation listed in Section A of Annex I. These guidelines shall be published at the latest on 1 August 2027.”;’

(b) paragraph 1, the second subparagraph is replaced by the following:

> ‘the application of the requirements and obligations referred to in Articles 8 to 15 and in Articles 25 and 26;
>
> ‘When issuing such guidelines, the Commission shall involve the AI Board and pay particular attention to the needs of SMEs, including start-ups, and SMCs, of local public authorities and of the sectors most likely to be affected by this Regulation.’;’’

*Transcription notes (not source text): (i) sub-point (a)'s quotation closes with a straight double quote — ”;’ — as printed; (ii) sub-point (b)'s replacement text opens with a stray line — "the application of the requirements and obligations referred to in Articles 8 to 15 and in Articles 25 and 26;" — that reads as a fragment of Article 96(1), point (a), followed by a second opening quote before "When issuing"; and it closes with a tripled quote sequence ’;’’ — all as printed; (iii) "the AI Board" — elsewhere "the Board" — as printed.*

▸ Extends the Commission's [point (28)](../commission/COM-2025-836_smc-simplifications.md#point-28--article-96-1-guidelines). **New versus the Commission proposal:** sub-point (a) adds a guidelines topic — the practical implementation of **Articles 8(2), 9(10) and 17(3)** as burden-minimising mechanisms for high-risk systems also covered by Section A of Annex I legislation, with a publication deadline of **1 August 2027** (cf. [recital (19a)](./PE-789081_recitals.md#recital-19a)) — duplicated in the apparent rewrite of the Article 96(1)(a) fragment in sub-point (b). The Commission's SMC addition to the guideline-audiences subparagraph is kept, now with a duty to **involve the AI Board**.

<a id="point-29--article-99-penalties"></a>
## Point (29) — Article 99 amended (penalties)

(29) Article 99 is amended as follows:

(a) paragraph 1 is replaced by the following:

> ‘1. In accordance with the terms and conditions laid down in this Regulation, Member States shall lay down the rules on penalties and other enforcement measures, which may also include administrative fines, warnings and non-monetary measures, applicable to any infringement of this Regulation by operators, and shall take all measures necessary to ensure that they are properly and effectively implemented, thereby taking into account the guidelines issued by the Commission pursuant to Article 96. The penalties provided for shall be effective, proportionate and dissuasive. The Member States shall take into account the interests of SMEs, including start-ups, and SMCs, and their economic viability when imposing penalties.’;’

(aa) in paragraph 4 the following point (da) is inserted:

> (da) 'obligations of providers and operators pursuant to Article 25(2) and (4); ’

(b) paragraph 6 is replaced by the following:

> ‘6. In the case of SMEs, including start-ups, each fine referred to in this Article shall be up to the percentages or amount referred to in paragraphs 3, 4 and 5, whichever thereof is lower.;’

*Transcription notes (not source text): (i) sub-point (aa)'s inserted point opens with a straight apostrophe ' and closes "; ’" with a space before the quote — as printed; (ii) paragraph 1 now reads "applicable to **any** infringement" and expressly lists "administrative fines" among the possible measures — changes versus the Commission text.*

▸ Builds on the Commission's [point (29)](../commission/COM-2025-836_smc-simplifications.md#point-29--article-99-penalties). Changes by the co-legislators: paragraph 1 expressly includes **administrative fines** among Member State enforcement measures and covers "**any** infringement"; new sub-point **(aa)** adds Article 99(4) point (da), making breaches of the **Article 25(2) and (4) value-chain cooperation obligations** (see [points (9a) and (9ab)](./PE-789081_final-provisions.md#point-9a--article-25-2-provider-switch)) finable in the EUR 15 million / 3 % band; paragraph 6 keeps the lower-of-the-two fine cap for **SMEs only** — the SMC cap is split out into the milder [point (29a)](#point-29a--article-99-6a-smc-fines).

<a id="point-29a--article-99-6a-smc-fines"></a>
## Point (29a) — Article 99, new paragraph 6a (SMC fine cap)

(29a) In Article 99, paragraph 6a is inserted:

> ‘In the case of SMCs each fine referred to in paragraphs 4 and 5 shall be up to the percentages or amount referred to in paragraph 4 or 5, whichever thereof is lower.’

*Transcription note (not source text): the inserted text does not carry the "6a." paragraph numeral — as printed.*

▸ **New — added by the co-legislators** (no counterpart in the Commission proposal, which had put SMCs in paragraph 6 alongside SMEs). A separate, narrower fine moderation for SMCs: the lower-of-the-two cap applies only to fines under paragraphs **4 and 5** (lower bands), not to paragraph 3 (Article 5 prohibition infringements) — unlike the SME cap in paragraph 6, which spans paragraphs 3, 4 and 5.
