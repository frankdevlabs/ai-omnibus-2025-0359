<a id="point-14--article-49-2-registration-deleted"></a>
## Point (14) — Article 49(2) deleted (registration of self-assessed non-high-risk systems) — [DELETED in PE789.081]

**[DELETED in PE789.081]** — the agreed text contains no point (14): the amending-point sequence runs (13) → (15). The Commission's deletion of Article 49(2) AI Act does not survive: the duty on providers who self-assess an Annex III system as **not** high-risk under Article 6(3) to register themselves and the system in the EU database is **retained**, with the information to be submitted simplified instead — see [point (32)](#point-32--annex-viii-section-b-deleted) and [recital (9)](./PE-789081_recitals.md#recital-9). The companion Commission change to Article 6(4) was likewise dropped — see [point (6)](./PE-789081_high-risk-classification-art6.md#point-6--article-6-4-documentation).

<a id="point-32--annex-viii-section-b-deleted"></a>
## Point (32) — Annex VIII, Section B, points 7 and 9 deleted

(32) in Annex VIII, section B, points 7 and 9 are deleted;

▸ Replaces the Commission's [point (32)](../commission/COM-2025-836_registration-art49-annexviii.md#point-32--annex-viii-section-b-deleted), which deleted Annex VIII **Section B in its entirety** (consequential to scrapping the Article 49(2) registration duty). The agreed text instead keeps Section B and the registration duty, deleting only **points 7 and 9** of Section B of the AI Act's Annex VIII (two of the information items to be submitted on registration — consult Regulation (EU) 2024/1689 for their content) — the "streamlining the required content" announced in [recital (9)](./PE-789081_recitals.md#recital-9). *(The same baseline anchor is kept for diff alignment although the agreed content is a partial, not full, deletion.)*
