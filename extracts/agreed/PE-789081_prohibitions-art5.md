## Point (5a) — Article 5 amended (prohibition of AI-generated non-consensual intimate material and CSAM)

(5a) Article 5 is amended as follows:

(a) the following points are added to paragraph 1, first subparagraph:

> (ba) the placing on the market, the putting into service or the use of an AI system that generates or manipulates realistic images, videos, audio or similar material of an identifiable natural person’s intimate parts, or of an identifiable natural person engaged in sexually explicit activities, without that person’s freely-given, specific, informed, unambiguous and explicit consent for that generation or manipulation;
>
> (bb) the placing on the market, the putting into service or the use of an AI system that generates or manipulates material or performance within the meaning of Article 2, points (c) and (e), of Directive 2011/93/EU, save where a ‘without right’ defence applies under national law;

(b) The following paragraph is inserted:

> 1a. For the purposes of paragraph 1, first subparagraph, points (ba) and (bb):
>
> (a) the placing on the market or putting into service of an AI system that generates or manipulates the material or performance referred to in points (ba) or (bb) above is only prohibited where:
>
> (i) that generation or manipulation is the intended purpose of the AI system; or
>
> (ii) the system’s design, training, architecture, capabilities or user-facing functionalities make that generation or manipulation a reasonably foreseeable reproducible outcome, without requiring significant technical modification, and the system does not have reasonable and adequate technical safety measures and other safeguards to reliably prevent that generation or manipulation, taking into account reasonably foreseeable misuse, and to correct observed or reported misuse.
>
> (b) the use of an AI system that generates or manipulates the material or performance referred to in points (ba) and (bb) above is only prohibited where the deployer uses the system for the purpose of generating or manipulating such material or performance.
>
> 1b. For the purposes of paragraph 1, first subparagraph, point (ba), an AI system that manipulates material in a way that does not increase the exposure of any depicted intimate parts or alter the nature of any depicted sexually explicit activities shall not constitute manipulation.’

*Transcription notes (not source text): (i) the inserted points (ba)/(bb) and paragraphs 1a/1b are printed without opening quote marks; a single closing quote appears after paragraph 1b — as printed; (ii) sub-instruction (b) opens with a capitalised "The" unlike sub-instruction (a) — as printed; (iii) point (b) of paragraph 1a uses "points (ba) and (bb)" where point (a) uses "points (ba) or (bb)" — as printed.*

▸ **New — added by the co-legislators** (no counterpart in the Commission proposal or in the current AI Act). Adds two prohibited practices to Article 5(1): **(ba)** AI generation/manipulation of realistic non-consensual intimate material of identifiable natural persons ("nudifier" applications), and **(bb)** AI generation/manipulation of child sexual abuse material/performance as defined by Directive 2011/93/EU, subject to a "without right" defence. New paragraph 1a confines the **provider-side** prohibition to two limbs — (i) intended purpose, or (ii) reasonably foreseeable reproducible outcome absent reasonable and adequate safeguards — and the **deployer-side** prohibition to purposeful use; new paragraph 1b carves out manipulations that do not increase exposure or alter the nature of depicted activity. Applies from **2 December 2026** via the amended [Article 113(3)(a)](./PE-789081_application-dates.md#point-31--article-113-application-dates). The recitals' detailed scoping (realism, consent mechanics, medical/red-teaming carve-outs, ne bis in idem) is in [recitals (6a)–(6d)](./PE-789081_recitals.md#recital-6a).
