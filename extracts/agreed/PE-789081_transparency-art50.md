<a id="point-15--article-50-7-codes-of-practice"></a>
## Point (15) — Article 50(7) replaced (codes of practice on detection, marking and labelling)

(15) in Article 50, paragraph 7 is replaced by the following:

> ‘7. The Commission shall encourage and facilitate the drawing up of codes of practice at Union level to facilitate the effective implementation of the obligations regarding the detection, marking and labelling of artificially generated or manipulated content. The Commission, taking utmost account of the opinion of the Board, shall assess whether adherence to those codes of practice is adequate to ensure compliance with the obligation laid down in paragraphs 2 and 4 of this Article, in accordance with the procedure laid down in Article 56(6). If it deems the code of practice is not adequate, the Commission may adopt an implementing act specifying common rules for the implementation of those obligations in accordance with the examination procedure laid down in Article 98(2).’;’

▸ Adjusts the Commission's [point (15)](../commission/COM-2025-836_transparency-art50.md#point-15--article-50-7-codes-of-practice): the facilitation duty moves from the AI Office back to the **Commission**, the adequacy assessment becomes mandatory ("shall assess", "taking utmost account of the opinion of the Board") and covers compliance with paragraphs **2 and 4** (the Commission text covered paragraph 2 only), and the cross-reference is to the Article 56(6) procedure as a whole rather than its first subparagraph. The implementing-act fallback where a code is deemed not adequate is kept. Versus current Article 50(7) AI Act, the "may approve by implementing act" mechanism remains replaced by assess-plus-fallback (cf. [recital (23)](./PE-789081_recitals.md#recital-23)).

<a id="point-16--article-56-6-codes-of-practice-assessment"></a>
## Point (16) — Article 56(6) replaced (assessment of GPAI codes of practice)

(16) in Article 56, paragraph 6 is replaced by the following:

> ‘6. The Commission and the Board shall regularly monitor and evaluate the achievement of the objectives of the codes of practice by the participants and their contribution to the proper application of this Regulation. The Commission, taking utmost account of the opinion of the Board, shall assess whether the codes of practice cover the obligations provided for in Articles 53 and 55, and shall regularly monitor and evaluate the achievement of their objectives. The Commission shall publish its assessment of the adequacy of the codes of practice.;’

▸ Same replacement text as the Commission's [point (16)](../commission/COM-2025-836_transparency-art50.md#point-16--article-56-6-codes-of-practice-assessment), but the amending instruction now replaces **paragraph 6 as a whole** rather than only its first subparagraph. Versus current Article 56(6) AI Act, the Commission's power to **approve** GPAI codes of practice by implementing act and give them general Union validity stays removed, replaced by an assess-and-publish mechanism; per [recital (23)](./PE-789081_recitals.md#recital-23), providers may rely under Articles 53(4) and 54(2) on codes assessed as adequate.
