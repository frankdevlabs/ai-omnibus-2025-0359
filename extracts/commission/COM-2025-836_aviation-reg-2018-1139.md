All seven points insert a materially identical new paragraph 3 into seven empowerment articles of
Regulation (EU) 2018/1139 (the EASA Basic Regulation), requiring that the high-risk AI
requirements of Chapter III, Section 2, of the AI Act be taken into account when the Commission
adopts implementing acts (points (1), (2), (4), (6), (7)) or delegated acts (points (3), (5))
concerning AI systems that are safety components.

## Article 2 — Amendments to Regulation (EU) 2018/1139

Regulation (EU) 2018/1139 is amended as follows:

<a id="art2-point-1--article-27"></a>
### Point (1) — Article 27 (implementing acts)

(1) in Article 27, the following paragraph is added:

> ‘3. Without prejudice to paragraph 2, when adopting implementing acts pursuant to paragraph 1 concerning Artificial Intelligence systems which are safety components within the meaning of Regulation (EU) 2024/1689 of the European Parliament and of the Council[^14], the requirements set out in Chapter III, Section 2, of that Regulation shall be taken into account.’;

▸ Article 27 of Regulation (EU) 2018/1139 concerns aircraft used by State-like operators (Article 2(5) aircraft); the new paragraph 3 binds future implementing acts to the AI Act's high-risk requirements.

<a id="art2-point-2--article-31"></a>
### Point (2) — Article 31 (implementing acts)

(2) in Article 31, the following paragraph is added:

> ‘3. Without prejudice to paragraph 2, when adopting implementing acts pursuant to paragraph 1 concerning Artificial Intelligence systems which are safety components within the meaning of Regulation (EU) 2024/1689 of the European Parliament and of the Council, the requirements set out in Chapter III, Section 2, of that Regulation shall be taken into account.’;

▸ Same insertion into Article 31 (air operations implementing acts).

<a id="art2-point-3--article-32"></a>
### Point (3) — Article 32 (delegated acts)

(3) in Article 32, the following paragraph is added:

> ‘3. When adopting delegated acts pursuant to paragraph 1 concerning Artificial Intelligence systems which are safety components within the meaning of Regulation (EU) 2024/1689 of the European Parliament and of the Council (*), the requirements set out in Chapter III, Section 2, of that Regulation shall be taken into account.’;

*Transcription note (not source text): the "(*)" footnote marker in point (3) has no corresponding footnote text anywhere in the proposal — as printed. Points (1) and (3) carry citation markers (footnote 14 and "(*)" respectively) while points (2), (4), (5), (6) and (7) cite the AI Act with no marker.*

▸ Same insertion into Article 32 (air-operations delegated acts); note no "Without prejudice to paragraph 2" proviso here, matching the delegated-act structure.

<a id="art2-point-4--article-36"></a>
### Point (4) — Article 36 (implementing acts)

(4) in Article 36, the following paragraph is added:

> ‘3. Without prejudice to paragraph 2, when adopting implementing acts pursuant to paragraph 1 concerning Artificial Intelligence systems which are safety components within the meaning of Regulation (EU) 2024/1689 of the European Parliament and of the Council, the requirements set out in Chapter III, Section 2, of that Regulation shall be taken into account.’;

▸ Same insertion into Article 36 (pilot training organisations etc.).

<a id="art2-point-5--article-39"></a>
### Point (5) — Article 39 (delegated acts)

(5) in Article 39 the following paragraph is added:

> ‘3. When adopting delegated acts pursuant to paragraph 1 concerning Artificial Intelligence systems which are safety components within the meaning of Regulation (EU) 2024/1689 of the European Parliament and of the Council, the requirements set out in Chapter III, Section 2, of that Regulation shall be taken into account.’;

*Transcription note (not source text): the amending instruction in point (5) omits the comma after "Article 39" used in the other points — as printed.*

▸ Same insertion into Article 39 (aerodromes delegated acts), again without the paragraph-2 proviso.

<a id="art2-point-6--article-50"></a>
### Point (6) — Article 50 (implementing acts)

(6) in Article 50, the following paragraph is added:

> ‘3. Without prejudice to paragraph 2, when adopting implementing acts pursuant to paragraph 1 concerning Artificial Intelligence systems which are safety components within the meaning of Regulation (EU) 2024/1689 of the European Parliament and of the Council, the requirements set out in Chapter III, Section 2, of that Regulation shall be taken into account.’;

▸ Same insertion into Article 50 (ATM/ANS implementing acts).

<a id="art2-point-7--article-53"></a>
### Point (7) — Article 53 (implementing acts)

(7) in Article 53, the following paragraph is added:

> ‘3. Without prejudice to paragraph 2, when adopting implementing acts pursuant to paragraph 1 concerning Artificial Intelligence systems which are safety components within the meaning of Regulation (EU) 2024/1689 of the European Parliament and of the Council, the requirements set out in Chapter III, Section 2, of that Regulation shall be taken into account.’.

▸ Same insertion into Article 53 (air traffic controllers); this final point closes with ’. rather than ’; in the source.
