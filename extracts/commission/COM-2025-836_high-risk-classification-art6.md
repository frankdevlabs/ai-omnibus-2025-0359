<a id="point-6--article-6-4-documentation"></a>
## Point (6) — Article 6(4) replaced (documentation of the non-high-risk self-assessment)

(6) in Article 6(4), paragraph 4 is replaced by the following:

> ‘4. A provider who considers that an AI system referred to in Annex III is not high-risk shall document its assessment before that system is placed on the market or put into service. Upon request of national competent authorities, the provider shall provide the documentation of the assessment.’;

*Transcription note (not source text): the amending instruction is redundantly worded in the source — "in Article 6(4), paragraph 4 is replaced" — reproduced as printed.*

▸ Replaces current Article 6(4) AI Act: drops the second sentence requiring the provider to **register** the self-assessed non-high-risk system pursuant to Article 49(2) (registration itself deleted by [point (14)](./COM-2025-836_registration-art49-annexviii.md#point-14--article-49-2-registration-deleted)), keeping only the documentation duty plus production on request.
