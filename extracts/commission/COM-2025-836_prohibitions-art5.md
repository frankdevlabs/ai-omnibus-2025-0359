## Article 5 of the AI Act — unchanged in this version

The Commission proposal COM(2025) 836 final **does not amend Article 5 of Regulation (EU)
2024/1689** (prohibited AI practices). None of the 33 amending points of Article 1 of the
proposal touches Article 5; the only references to it in the proposal's operative text are
incidental ("without prejudice to the prohibitions under Article 5" in the real-world-testing
provisions — see [points (19) and (20)](./COM-2025-836_sandboxes-testing-art57.md#point-19--article-60-real-world-testing) —
and "Without prejudice to the application of Article 5 as referred to in Article 113(3), third
paragraph, point (a)" in [point (30)](./COM-2025-836_application-dates.md#point-30--article-111-transition)).

▸ The new Article 5 prohibition on nudifier apps / AI-generated CSAM was **added later by the
co-legislators** during the legislative procedure — it is not part of the Commission baseline.
