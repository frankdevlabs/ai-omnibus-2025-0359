<a id="point-14--article-49-2-registration-deleted"></a>
## Point (14) — Article 49(2) deleted (registration of self-assessed non-high-risk systems)

(14) in Article 49, paragraph 2 is deleted;

▸ Removes the duty on providers who self-assess an Annex III system as **not** high-risk under Article 6(3) to register themselves and the system in the EU database before placing it on the market. The documentation duty remains via the amended Article 6(4) — see [point (6)](./COM-2025-836_high-risk-classification-art6.md#point-6--article-6-4-documentation).

<a id="point-32--annex-viii-section-b-deleted"></a>
## Point (32) — Annex VIII, Section B deleted

(32) in Annex VIII, section B is deleted;

▸ Consequential to [point (14)](#point-14--article-49-2-registration-deleted): deletes Annex VIII Section B of the AI Act, the list of information to be submitted upon registration of those self-assessed non-high-risk AI systems under Article 49(2).
