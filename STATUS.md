## One-line status

Council and Parliament reached a **provisional political agreement on 7 May 2026** (negotiators'
compromise 6 May; Coreper confirmed 13 May; IMCO/LIBE approved the text 2 Jun); the **EP adopted its
1st-reading text `T10-0198/2026` on 16 Jun 2026** (debate 15 Jun), the plenary adoption of the agreed
text PE789.081. The **Council adopted the act at 1st reading on 29 Jun 2026** (final green light,
approving the EP position) — completing the procedure. The **final act was signed by the two Presidents
on 8 Jul 2026** and **published in the Official Journal on 24 Jul 2026 as Regulation (EU) 2026/1744**
(CELEX 32026R1744; OEIL Status "Procedure completed"). It **entered into force on 27 Jul 2026** (third
day after publication) — comfortably before the 2 Aug 2026 AI Act high-risk backstop. The file is closed.

## What changed in the latest text vs earlier reporting

Each row traces a provision **current law → Commission proposal (COM(2025) 836) → agreed text
(PE789.081)** — the agreed text now being the adopted **Regulation (EU) 2026/1744** (in force 27 Jul
2026), which consolidates it — so it is clear *which institution changed what*. This is the headline
set; the full provision-by-provision table is in [`docs/what-changed.md`](docs/what-changed.md).
Operative text: [extracts](extracts/) · positions: [`data/positions.csv`](data/positions.csv).

| Provision | Current law → Commission proposal (19 Nov 2025) | Agreed text (PE789.081, 13 May 2026) |
|---|---|---|
| [**High-risk application dates (Art 6 / Annexes I+III)**](docs/provisions/high-risk-classification-and-dates.md) | *AI Act:* Annex III obligations apply **2 Aug 2026**, Annex I **2 Aug 2027**. **Commission** proposed a *conditional* mechanism (application tied to availability of standards/tools, with backstop dates — "up to 16 months") | **Fixed dates**: Annex III → **2 Dec 2027**, Annex I → **2 Aug 2028** (a Council-mandate change carried through). General application date of 2 Aug 2026 otherwise stands |
| [**Art 5 prohibition — nudifiers / CSAM**](docs/provisions/prohibitions-art5-nudifiers-csam.md) | *Not in current law as such; NOT in the Commission proposal* | **New Art 5 ban** (EP + Council addition): AI systems generating CSAM or non-consensual intimate imagery; three-limb structure incl. providers where generation is "reasonably foreseeable and reproducible"; applies **2 Dec 2026**; fines up to €35m / 7% |
| [**Registration (Art 49 / Annex VIII)**](docs/provisions/registration-art49.md) | *AI Act:* providers self-assessing Annex III systems as non-high-risk must register. **Commission** proposed to **scrap** that duty | **Restored** by both co-legislators (per EDPB/EDPS warning), with simplified information requirements (Annex VIII Section B) |
| [**Special-category data for bias detection**](docs/provisions/bias-data-art4a.md) | *AI Act Art 10(5):* "strictly necessary", high-risk only. **Commission** proposed lowering to "necessary" and extending to all systems and deployers | **"Strict necessity" restored**; extension beyond high-risk only "by exception" (health/safety/fundamental-rights bias), explicitly no duty to detect bias |
| [**Watermarking transition (Art 50(2))**](docs/provisions/transparency-art50-watermarking.md) | *AI Act:* applies 2 Aug 2026. **Commission** proposed a 6-month grace period (to 2 Feb 2027) for systems already on the market | Grace period **shortened to 4 months** — transition until **2 Dec 2026** (EP had pushed ~3 months) |
| [**AI literacy (Art 4)**](docs/provisions/ai-literacy-art4.md) | *AI Act:* binding duty on providers/deployers (applies since 2 Feb 2025). **Commission** (and Council) wanted an encouragement framework led by Commission/Member States instead | **Obligation restored** on providers/deployers, lowered from "ensure a sufficient level" to *"take measures to **support the development**"* of AI literacy (verified against PE789.081); Commission/Member-State support + practical examples |
