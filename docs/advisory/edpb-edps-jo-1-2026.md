The most consequential outside input on this file: its three core asks were all reflected in the
Council mandate and the EP position, and carried into the provisional agreement.

## Core positions

1. **Bias-detection data (Art 10(5) / new Art 4a):** restore the **"strict necessity"** standard the
   Commission had lowered to "necessary" — taken up by both co-legislators.
   → [`../provisions/bias-data-art4a.md`](../provisions/bias-data-art4a.md)
2. **Registration:** do not scrap the duty to register self-assessed non-high-risk Annex III
   systems — dropping it would create *"an undesirable incentive ... to unduly invoke this
   exemption"*. Restored by Council and EP.
   → [`../provisions/registration-art49.md`](../provisions/registration-art49.md)
3. **Timeline:** *"They advocate for maintaining the current timeline, and if this proves
   unfeasible, for minimising any delay in the extent possible."* — the one ask that did **not**
   prevail: the agreement fixes Annex III at 2 Dec 2027 and Annex I at 2 Aug 2028.
   → [`../provisions/high-risk-classification-and-dates.md`](../provisions/high-risk-classification-and-dates.md)

Overall posture: support for genuine simplification, warning against weakening fundamental-rights
safeguards under a simplification label.
