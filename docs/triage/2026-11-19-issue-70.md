## What was flagged
New RSS item (19 Nov 2026) matching `Digital Omnibus` — marks the one-year point of the 19 Nov 2025
Digital Omnibus package. EDRi publishes a coalition statement: "100 organisations and individuals from
across civil society" warning that "simplification is turning into deregulation", with an open letter to
the European Parliament and Member States. The flag reads it as a package-level anniversary item.

Confirmed against the live source and the linked coalition letter PDF
(`edri.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/201611-letter-Digital-Omnibus-anniversary.pdf`, fetched
2026-11-19):

- The letter is titled against **the "Digital Omnibus on Data"** and is "deeply concerned about the
  direction of the so-called 'Digital Omnibus on Data' and the weakening of fundamental rights
  protections" — i.e. **data protection and confidentiality of communications**.
- Every substantive ask concerns the **GDPR and ePrivacy** instruments (the letter: "These reforms
  concern the GDPR and ePrivacy rules, which implement rights protected by Articles 7 and 8 of the
  Charter"): reject a redefinition of personal data "via Article 4 GDPR"; reject "privileged routes for
  large-scale reuse of personal data for AI" (the GDPR legitimate-interest route); automated
  decision-making safeguards; device-access (ePrivacy) rules.
- The only AI-flavoured content is the **"AI data grab"** — the GDPR legitimate-interest legal basis for
  AI development/training/operation. That is a **GDPR amendment**, carried in the Data Omnibus, not an
  AI Act amendment.
- The letter makes **no mention** of the AI Act, the AI-Omnibus's high-risk application-date
  postponements, the Art 5 nudifier/CSAM prohibition, Art 50(2) watermarking, registration/bias-data
  rules, SMC simplifications or the aviation amendments — i.e. **none** of the provisions this repo
  tracks.
- noyb is not named on the page; EDRi is the publisher. Signatory list is in the letter PDF (not
  enumerated on the page).
