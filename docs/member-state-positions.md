## Netherlands

**Government answer to written questions on deepfake pornography of female politicians** — Minister Van
Weel (Justice and Security), also for the Minister of the Interior, received 23 Nov 2026 (*Aanhangsel
Handelingen II* 2026/27, **nr. 737**; questions 2026Z21771 by Kröger and 14 other women MPs across eight
parties) — [register](../sources/README.md) · [officielebekendmakingen.nl](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/ah-1281565.html).

The answer is the first NL government statement in this repo touching the file's central AI-Act addition,
the [**new Art 5 nudifier/CSAM prohibition**](provisions/prohibitions-art5-nudifiers-csam.md) (adopted
via Regulation (EU) 2026/1744, applying 2 Dec 2026). Position points:

- **Scope — reads the ban broadly.** The cabinet states the Art 5 ban on placing-on-market / putting-into-
  service / use covers "all AI systems often referred to as 'nudifier apps'" *regardless of the channel*
  by which they are offered or reachable, **including via so-called gateway sites**, and covers
  **open-weight models** (classification does not turn on the underlying model). It expressly notes these
  scope questions "were **not part of the negotiation talks on the Omnibus**" — i.e. read into the adopted
  text, not negotiated in 2025/0359.
- **Sequencing — no new EU rules yet.** On proposals for consent-based model-level guardrails, the cabinet
  wants the AI Act "first fully in force and then effectively enforced before new rules are introduced."
- **Enforcement / national implementation.** The EU **AI Office** enforces directly from **December 2026**
  for systems built on GPAI by the same provider, or forming/integrated into a VLOP/VLOSE (Reg. 2022/2065);
  otherwise national market-surveillance applies. The intended national authorities are designated by the
  **draft AI Act implementing law** (*uitvoeringswet AI-verordening*, out of consultation, being readied
  for Council of State advice); the **Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens (AP)** is the intended authority for
  prohibited AI practices and can already act under the GDPR against non-consensual deepfakes.
- **Adjacent, non-AI-Act measures** (noted for context, outside this file): DSA duties on hosts/VLOPs/
  VLOSEs; a push for an EU-level mandatory **KYC** duty on hosting providers; existing criminal-law
  offences (Arts 252 and 254ba Dutch Criminal Code covering AI-generated sexual imagery of minors and
  adults); and a forthcoming cabinet analysis on tackling sexual deepfakes (letter of 16 Oct 2026).

*Not a negotiating position on 2025/0359 (the file is closed) — a national read of the adopted text and
its domestic enforcement.*
