# Amended instruments

This file amends two regulations (per [`tracker.yaml`](../../tracker.yaml) `instruments`):

## AI Act — Regulation (EU) 2024/1689

The substantive target. Article 1 of the omnibus carries the amendments as numbered points; this
repo's analysis is organised by theme in [`../provisions/`](../provisions/):
[application dates](../provisions/high-risk-classification-and-dates.md) ·
[Art 5 ban](../provisions/prohibitions-art5-nudifiers-csam.md) ·
[Art 4 literacy](../provisions/ai-literacy-art4.md) ·
[bias data](../provisions/bias-data-art4a.md) ·
[registration](../provisions/registration-art49.md) ·
[transparency](../provisions/transparency-art50-watermarking.md) ·
[sandboxes](../provisions/sandboxes-testing-art57.md) ·
[governance](../provisions/governance-aioffice-art75.md) ·
[SMC](../provisions/smc-simplifications.md) ·
[Annex I interaction](../provisions/sectoral-annexi-interaction.md).

The AI Act's architecture (risk-based approach, four risk tiers, GPAI track, AI Office) is **not**
reopened. Until the omnibus is in the OJ, the unamended AI Act remains the law in force.

## EASA Basic Regulation — Regulation (EU) 2018/1139 (aviation)

Article 2 of the omnibus; technical integration of the AI Act high-risk requirements into the
aviation safety framework. → [`../provisions/aviation-reg-2018-1139.md`](../provisions/aviation-reg-2018-1139.md)

## Referenced, not amended

The new Art 5 prohibition interfaces with CSAM law; the bias-data provision interfaces with the
**GDPR (2016/679)**; the machinery equivalence clause interfaces with the **Machinery Regulation**;
AI Office competence over VLOPs/VLOSEs references the **DSA**. None of these instruments is amended
by this file — the GDPR is amended by the *sibling* file 2025/0360 (COD), tracked separately.
