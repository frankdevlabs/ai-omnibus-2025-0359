# Governance — AI Office powers (Art 75)

Operative text: [Commission](../../extracts/commission/COM-2025-836_governance-aioffice-art75.md) ·
[agreed](../../extracts/agreed/PE-789081_governance-aioffice-art75.md)

The agreement strengthens the **AI Office** with partly **exclusive competence** over:

- AI systems built on **GPAI models where model and system come from the same provider** (or group);
- AI in **VLOPs / VLOSEs** (very large online platforms / search engines, DSA categories).

**National authorities stay competent** for, among others, law enforcement, border management,
judicial authorities and financial institutions (the ECB's interest —
[CON/2026/10 digest](../advisory/ecb-con-2026-10.md)).

New enforcement instruments for the AI Office: investigations, on-site inspections, binding
commitments, fines.

This goes beyond the Commission proposal's governance content and was part of the final package
trade-off. Watch the practical Commission/Member-State boundary-drawing once implementation starts.
