# AI literacy (Art 4)

Operative text: [Commission](../../extracts/commission/COM-2025-836_ai-literacy-art4.md) ·
[agreed](../../extracts/agreed/PE-789081_ai-literacy-art4.md)

| Lane | Position |
|---|---|
| **Current law (AI Act Art 4)** | Binding duty on providers/deployers to **ensure a sufficient level** of AI literacy of their staff — **already applies since 2 Feb 2025** |
| **Commission** | Replace with an encouragement framework led by Commission and Member States (shift "from the organisation to the Member States") |
| **Council (ST 7322/26)** | Followed the Commission shift |
| **Parliament** | **Restored** the provider/deployer obligation at a lowered standard, plus Commission support duties |
| **Agreed (PE789.081)** | Providers/deployers *"shall take **measures to support the development** of AI literacy"* (verified against the transcribed text) — softer than the current "ensure a sufficient level"; with a no-guarantee clause, Commission/Member-State support incl. practical examples via the Art 62(3)(d) platform, and Board recommendations. No "public-private partnerships" wording (a claim from secondary reporting that is **not** in the text) |

Wording verified against the transcribed agreed text — still **pending legal-linguistic revision**;
re-verify against the adopted text at plenary/OJ stage and update this page and
[`../what-changed.md`](../what-changed.md). Note the oddity that Art 4 has been applicable law since
Feb 2025 — organisations that built literacy programmes face a *retroactive softening*.
