# Transparency and watermarking (Art 50)

Operative text: [Commission](../../extracts/commission/COM-2025-836_transparency-art50.md) ·
[agreed](../../extracts/agreed/PE-789081_transparency-art50.md)

| Lane | Position |
|---|---|
| **Current law (AI Act Art 50(2))** | Machine-readable marking of synthetic content applies from **2 Aug 2026** |
| **Commission** | 6-month grace period for systems already on the market before that date — until **2 Feb 2027** |
| **Parliament** | Cut to ~3 months (Covington: "until 2 November 2026") |
| **Agreed (PE789.081)** | **4-month transition — until 2 Dec 2026** for systems placed on the market before 2 Aug 2026 |

The **other Art 50 transparency duties are unchanged** (deployer disclosure, informing people they
interact with AI) and apply from 2 Aug 2026 as scheduled. Same compliance date (2 Dec 2026) as the
new [Art 5 prohibition](prohibitions-art5-nudifiers-csam.md) — the two are the near-term compliance
cliff while the high-risk regime waits until [Dec 2027 / Aug 2028](high-risk-classification-and-dates.md).
