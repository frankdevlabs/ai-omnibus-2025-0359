# High-risk classification and application dates (Art 6, Annexes I + III)

**The core of the file.** Operative text: [Commission](../../extracts/commission/COM-2025-836_high-risk-classification-art6.md) ·
[agreed](../../extracts/agreed/PE-789081_high-risk-classification-art6.md) · dates:
[Commission](../../extracts/commission/COM-2025-836_application-dates.md) ·
[agreed](../../extracts/agreed/PE-789081_application-dates.md)

| Lane | Position |
|---|---|
| **Current law (AI Act)** | Annex III stand-alone high-risk obligations apply **2 Aug 2026**; Annex I embedded systems **2 Aug 2027** |
| **Commission (COM(2025) 836)** | *Conditional* mechanism: application tied to availability of harmonised standards / support tools, with backstop dates (Council framing: delay "by up to 16 months") |
| **Council (ST 7322/26)** | Replace with **fixed dates** |
| **Parliament (T10-0098/2026)** | Fixed dates too; also pushed to narrow Annex I (see [sectoral interaction](sectoral-annexi-interaction.md)) |
| **Agreed (PE789.081)** | **Annex III → 2 Dec 2027; Annex I → 2 Aug 2028.** General application date of 2 Aug 2026 stands; AI Act architecture (risk tiers, GPAI track, AI Office role) intact |

Also agreed: the **"safety component" definition is narrowed** — AI that merely supports users or
optimises performance falls outside high-risk unless its failure endangers health/safety.

**Lex lata caveat:** until OJ publication the original dates remain law — Annex III formally still
applies from 2 Aug 2026. The EDPB/EDPS had asked to keep the timeline or minimise delay
([digest](../advisory/edpb-edps-jo-1-2026.md)); the fixed-date delay went through regardless.
