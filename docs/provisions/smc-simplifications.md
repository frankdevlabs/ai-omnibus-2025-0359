# SME reliefs extended to small mid-caps (SMCs)

Operative text: [Commission](../../extracts/commission/COM-2025-836_smc-simplifications.md) ·
[agreed](../../extracts/agreed/PE-789081_smc-simplifications.md)

The AI Act's SME accommodations — **simplified technical documentation**, a **proportionate
quality-management system**, **proportionate fines** — are extended to **small mid-caps (SMCs)**, a
new company category. Definitions in the operative text cite Recommendation 2003/361/EC and
Recommendation (EU) 2025/1099 (the "2025/3500/EC" reference circulating in secondary reporting
appears only as a recital citation quirk — flagged in the extracts).

Part of the Commission's original simplification core that survived the negotiation intact
(see [commission-proposal digest](../commission-proposal.md)). Uncontested between the
institutions; mainly relevant for compliance scoping once the [postponed high-risk
dates](high-risk-classification-and-dates.md) arrive.
