# Aviation — amendments to Regulation (EU) 2018/1139

Operative text: [Commission](../../extracts/commission/COM-2025-836_aviation-reg-2018-1139.md) ·
[agreed](../../extracts/agreed/PE-789081_aviation-reg-2018-1139.md)

Article 2 of the omnibus makes **technical adjustments** to the EASA Basic Regulation (EU)
2018/1139 to integrate the AI Act's high-risk requirements consistently into the aviation safety
framework — the aviation-specific counterpart of the horizontal
[sectoral-interaction](sectoral-annexi-interaction.md) solution.

Reason this file amends two regulations (and why TRAN gave an opinion, PE785.326). Low-controversy
throughout the procedure; verify the final Article 2 text against the consolidated version once
adopted. Instrument page: [`../instruments/related-instruments.md`](../instruments/related-instruments.md).
