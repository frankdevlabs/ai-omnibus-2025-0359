# Regulatory sandboxes and real-world testing (Art 57 ff.)

Operative text: [Commission](../../extracts/commission/COM-2025-836_sandboxes-testing-art57.md) ·
[agreed](../../extracts/agreed/PE-789081_sandboxes-testing-art57.md)

| Item | Current law (AI Act) | Agreed (PE789.081) |
|---|---|---|
| National sandbox deadline | At least one per Member State by 2 Aug 2026 | Moved to **2 Aug 2027** |
| Real-world testing outside sandboxes | Limited | **Broadened**, extended to Annex I systems |
| EU-level sandbox | — | Possibility for the AI Office to establish one (Art 57(3a)) — **no date in the text**; the "from 2028" in secondary reporting is not in PE789.081 |
| Post-market monitoring | Prescribed harmonised plan | **More flexibility** (no prescribed harmonised plan) |

Innovation-track counterpart to the [high-risk delay](high-risk-classification-and-dates.md): the
support infrastructure shifts out roughly in step with the postponed obligations.
