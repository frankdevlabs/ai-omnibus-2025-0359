# Article 5 prohibition — nudification apps / AI-generated CSAM

**New ban, added by the co-legislators — not in the Commission proposal.** Operative text:
[agreed](../../extracts/agreed/PE-789081_prohibitions-art5.md) (no Commission counterpart slice —
the proposal did not touch Art 5).

Prohibits AI systems that generate child sexual abuse material (CSAM), or that depict the intimate
parts of an identifiable person without consent or show that person in sexually explicit acts
(image, video, audio). Three-limb structure:

1. **Placing on the market** with that purpose;
2. **Placing on the market without reasonable safeguards** preventing such generation — for
   providers the ban extends to systems where such generation is a **"reasonably foreseeable and
   reproducible"** result absent significant technical modification and the system lacks reasonable
   safeguards;
3. **Use by a deployer** for that purpose.

- **Compliance date:** **2 Dec 2026** (earlier than the postponed high-risk dates).
- **Sanctions:** Art 5 breaches carry the top fine band — up to **€35m or 7%** of worldwide annual
  turnover.
- **Origin:** Council mandate (13 Mar 2026) + EP mandate, after the Grok mass-deepfake episode of
  early Jan 2026. Kokalari (7 May): "we do not have a list of nudifier apps, but we all know what
  Grok and X did a few months ago, and we want to send the message that all of this is banned in
  Europe." McNamara called it "a key part of the Parliament's mandate" (EP press 20260427IPR42011).
- **Watch:** exact placement/numbering within Art 5 and the scope of the provider limb are based on
  the EP mandate and secondary analyses — **verify against the consolidated text** (pending
  legal-linguistic revision).
