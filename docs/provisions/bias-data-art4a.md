# Special-category data for bias detection (new Art 4a; GDPR interaction)

Operative text: [Commission](../../extracts/commission/COM-2025-836_bias-data-art4a.md) ·
[agreed](../../extracts/agreed/PE-789081_bias-data-art4a.md)

| Lane | Position |
|---|---|
| **Current law (AI Act Art 10(5))** | Special-category personal data may be processed for bias detection/correction where **strictly necessary**, high-risk systems only, with safeguards |
| **Commission** | Lower the threshold to "**necessary**"; extend the legal basis to **all** AI systems/models and to **deployers** |
| **EDPB–EDPS (JO 1/2026)** | Restore "strict necessity" |
| **Council + Parliament** | Restored **"strict necessity"**; extension beyond high-risk only **"by exception"** — where bias affects health/safety/fundamental rights or leads to prohibited discrimination — with the clarification that **no obligation to detect bias** arises |
| **Agreed (PE789.081)** | The Council/EP line |
