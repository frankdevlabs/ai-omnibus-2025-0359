# Fault lines — the contested issues

> The issues that dominated the negotiation, who stood where, and what remains open. Per-provision
> detail: [`provisions/`](provisions/). Positions overview:
> [`institutional-positions.md`](institutional-positions.md).

- **Annex I sectoral conformity assessment — the issue that broke a trilogue.** The 28 Apr 2026
  political trilogue collapsed after ~12 hours over conformity assessment for AI embedded in Annex I
  products (machinery, medical devices, toys…). The EP pushed to go further (deleting Annex I
  Section A); BEUC and consumer groups warned that would take toys and medical devices out of the
  AI Act. Landed as: machinery products move to sectoral safety rules under an **equivalence
  clause**, with Commission delegated/implementing-act powers for other sectors.
  → [`provisions/sectoral-annexi-interaction.md`](provisions/sectoral-annexi-interaction.md)

- **Delay vs deregulation.** Civil society (EDRi, noyb, BEUC, CDT) and the EDPB/EDPS accepted
  simplification rhetoric but fought the substantive easements (registration, "strict necessity",
  Art 4). Most of those easements were reversed; the *delay itself* (Annex III to Dec 2027) went
  through against the EDPB/EDPS preference to keep the timeline. Post-deal (11 Jun 2026), EDRi,
  Access Now, ECNL and Amnesty International issued a joint statement urging the Parliament to
  **reject** the deal at the 15 Jun plenary as a "rollback of AI safeguards". Post-adoption (16 Jul
  2026), EDRi's retrospective maintains the line — the adopted text "does more than delay the AI
  Act", weakening it "before key safeguards fully apply" — closing the civil-society arc without a
  new procedural fact.
  → [`stakeholders.md`](stakeholders.md) · [`advisory/edpb-edps-jo-1-2026.md`](advisory/edpb-edps-jo-1-2026.md)

- **The nudifier/CSAM ban — Parliament's price.** Not in the Commission proposal; added by Council
  mandate and EP position after the Grok mass-deepfake episode of early Jan 2026 (Kokalari, 7 May:
  "we do not have a list of nudifier apps, but we all know what Grok and X did a few months ago").
  Scope of the provider limb ("reasonably foreseeable and reproducible" generation) is the part to
  watch in the final wording. → [`provisions/prohibitions-art5-nudifiers-csam.md`](provisions/prohibitions-art5-nudifiers-csam.md)

- **Art 4 AI literacy — restored, softened.** Commission and Council wanted the duty moved to
  Member States; the EP restored it on providers/deployers at a lowered standard — the agreed text
  reads "take measures to **support the development** of AI literacy" (verified against PE789.081;
  still subject to legal-linguistic revision).
  → [`provisions/ai-literacy-art4.md`](provisions/ai-literacy-art4.md)

- **The 2 Aug 2026 cliff.** Everything depends on adoption + OJ publication before 2 Aug 2026. If
  the plenary (indicative 15 Jun) or Council adoption slips past mid-July, the original AI Act
  timeline formally bites and the repo must flag the scenario. → `STATUS.md` "Next milestones".
