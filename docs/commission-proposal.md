> Digest of the Commission proposal's explanatory memorandum (**COM(2025) 836 final**, 19 Nov 2025).
> The operative articles live in [`extracts/commission/`](../extracts/commission/) (the diff
> baseline). What the co-legislators changed: [`what-changed.md`](what-changed.md).

- **Proposal:** `COM(2025) 836 final` (2025-11-19), accompanied by SWD(2025) 836
- **Base-text extracts:** [`extracts/commission/`](../extracts/commission/)
- **Register entry:** [`sources/README.md`](../sources/README.md)
- **Legal basis:** Art 114 TFEU; amends Regulation (EU) 2024/1689 (AI Act) and Regulation (EU) 2018/1139 (aviation)

## Why the Commission says it is acting

Part of the 19 Nov 2025 **Digital Omnibus package** (with the data/cyber proposal COM(2025) 837,
the European Business Wallets proposal and the Digital Fitness Check). The AI strand was split into
its own COD file and fast-tracked for one reason: the AI Act's high-risk obligations would apply
from **2 August 2026**, while harmonised standards, common specifications and Commission guidance
were running late. The memorandum frames the file as *simplification and implementation-readiness*,
not reopening the AI Act's substance.

## What the Commission proposed (headline items)

1. **Conditional postponement of high-risk application** — application of the Annex III / Annex I
   high-risk regimes tied to the availability of standards and support tools, with backstop dates
   (described by the Council as a delay "by up to 16 months").
2. **Registration easement** — scrap the duty to register Annex III systems the provider
   self-assesses as *non*-high-risk (narrow/procedural tasks).
3. **Bias-detection data easement** — lower the Art 10(5) threshold for processing special-category
   data from "strictly necessary" to "necessary", extended to all AI systems/models and deployers.
4. **AI literacy (Art 4)** — replace the binding provider/deployer duty with an encouragement
   framework led by Commission and Member States.
5. **Watermarking transition (Art 50(2))** — six-month grace period (to 2 Feb 2027) for systems
   already on the market.
6. **SME reliefs** — simplified technical documentation and proportionate quality-management
   obligations.
7. **Sectoral coherence** — adjustments for AI embedded in regulated products (Annex I), and
   technical integration of AI Act requirements into the aviation framework (Reg 2018/1139).

## What survived

Items 1 (as **fixed dates** instead of a conditional mechanism), 5 (shortened to 4 months), 6
(extended to small mid-caps) and 7 (reworked into an equivalence clause + empowerments). Items 2–4
were **reversed or restored** by Council and Parliament, following the EDPB/EDPS Joint Opinion
1/2026 — see [`institutional-positions.md`](institutional-positions.md) and
[`what-changed.md`](what-changed.md). The Art 5 nudifier/CSAM prohibition was **not** in the
proposal at all; it is a co-legislator addition.
