# Summary — what this file does

The **Digital Omnibus on AI** ("AI Omnibus", file 2025/0359 (COD), proposal COM(2025) 836 of
19 November 2025) amends the **AI Act** (Regulation (EU) 2024/1689) before most of its high-risk
rules ever start applying, plus makes technical AI-related changes to the aviation Regulation (EU)
2018/1139. It is the AI half of the Commission's Digital Omnibus simplification package — the
data/privacy half (COM(2025) 837, file 2025/0360 (COD)) is a separate, slower file tracked in the
sibling repo.

The headline, as politically agreed on 7 May 2026: the high-risk obligations are **postponed**
(stand-alone Annex III systems to **2 December 2027**, AI embedded in regulated products under
Annex I to **2 August 2028**), while the AI Act's architecture — risk tiers, GPAI rules, AI Office —
stays intact. In exchange for the delay, the co-legislators **added protections** the Commission had
not proposed: a new Article 5 **ban on "nudification" apps and AI-generated child sexual abuse
material** (applying 2 December 2026), restored registration duties, the restored "strict necessity"
standard for using sensitive data in bias detection, and only a short (4-month) transition for
watermarking synthetic content. SME reliefs are extended to small mid-caps, the AI Office gains
enforcement powers, and double conformity assessment is removed for machinery products under an
equivalence clause.

The file was fast-tracked to get into the Official Journal **before 2 August 2026**, when the
original high-risk timeline would bite. Where it stands right now: `STATUS.md`. Detail per theme:
[`provisions/`](provisions/). What actually changed vs the proposal: [`what-changed.md`](what-changed.md).
