# Institutional positions — Commission vs Council vs Parliament vs agreement

> Co-legislator comparison. Per-provision detail in [`data/positions.csv`](../data/positions.csv)
> and [`provisions/`](provisions/); the one-screen current state is `STATUS.md`.

| Institution | Stage | Headline position |
|---|---|---|
| **Commission** | proposer | Simplify + delay conditionally; ease registration, bias-data and literacy duties ([digest](commission-proposal.md)) |
| **Council** | co-legislator — mandate ST 7322/26 (13 Mar 2026) | Fixed dates instead of conditional delay; added the nudifier/CSAM ban; restored registration + "strict necessity" |
| **Parliament** | co-legislator — position T10-0098/2026 (26 Mar 2026, 569/45/23) | Same restorations; nudifier/CSAM ban "a key part of the Parliament's mandate"; restored (softened) Art 4 duty; shorter watermarking transition |
| **Agreement** | provisional (7 May 2026; text PE789.081) | Council-style fixed dates + EP-style protections — see [`what-changed.md`](what-changed.md) |

## How the positions converged, per theme

- **High-risk dates:** Commission conditional mechanism → Council fixed dates (Annex III 2 Dec 2027,
  Annex I 2 Aug 2028) → agreement keeps the fixed dates.
  [`provisions/high-risk-classification-and-dates.md`](provisions/high-risk-classification-and-dates.md)
- **Art 5 nudifier/CSAM ban:** absent from the proposal → introduced by the Council mandate and the
  EP position (post-Grok) → agreed, applying 2 Dec 2026.
  [`provisions/prohibitions-art5-nudifiers-csam.md`](provisions/prohibitions-art5-nudifiers-csam.md)
- **Registration:** Commission scrapped it → Council and EP both restored it (EDPB/EDPS line), with
  simplified Annex VIII Section B requirements → agreed.
  [`provisions/registration-art49.md`](provisions/registration-art49.md)
- **Bias data:** Commission "necessary" for all systems/deployers → Council and EP restored "strictly
  necessary", non-high-risk only by exception, no detection duty → agreed.
  [`provisions/bias-data-art4a.md`](provisions/bias-data-art4a.md)
- **Art 4 literacy:** Commission + Council → Member-State-led encouragement; EP → restored duty at a
  lowered standard. Agreement: providers/deployers "take measures to support the development" of AI
  literacy (verified). [`provisions/ai-literacy-art4.md`](provisions/ai-literacy-art4.md)
- **Watermarking:** Commission 6-month grace → EP ~3 months → agreed 4 months (to 2 Dec 2026).
  [`provisions/transparency-art50-watermarking.md`](provisions/transparency-art50-watermarking.md)
- **Annex I sectoral interaction:** the trilogue-breaker (28 Apr). EP wanted Annex I Section A
  deleted; landed on the machinery equivalence clause + Commission empowerments.
  [`provisions/sectoral-annexi-interaction.md`](provisions/sectoral-annexi-interaction.md)
- **Governance:** agreement strengthens the AI Office (partly exclusive competence over GPAI-built
  systems by the same provider and AI in VLOPs/VLOSEs, new enforcement tools).
  [`provisions/governance-aioffice-art75.md`](provisions/governance-aioffice-art75.md)
